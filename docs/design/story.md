# Story

Text screens use the menu font (`hud.md`) on a hand-drawn papyrus panel, max 36 characters per
line, max 12 lines per screen. Lines below are already wrapped to fit.

## Premise

Catlantis has been ruled by cats for nine thousand years (they count in lives). Last night the
Kennel Dominion, dog-men who replaced their better judgment with servo-motors, marched out of the
desert and occupied every temple, museum and kibble silo. Their overlord, Pharaoh-Overlord Woofu,
plans to grind the Great Sphinx's face into a dog's and broadcast one command to every cat in the
land: SIT. One cat slept through the entire invasion. She is awake now.

## Intermissions (after levels 1-7, shown below the tally)

1. `THE GATE IS OPEN. INSIDE, THE MEW-SEUM IS BEING LOOTED. NOBODY LOOTS THE MUMMIFIED MICE. THOSE
   ARE FOR LATER.`
2. `THE MUSEUM IS SAFE. THE GIFT SHOP IS NOT. MAU TAKES A SOUVENIR SCATTERGUN AND HEADS FOR THE
   DOCKS, WHERE THE DOGS ARE PUMPING THE NILE INTO SOMETHING CALLED "GRAVY".`
3. `THE PUMPS ARE DOWN. A CAPTURED POMERANIAN, BRIBED WITH A BRUSHING, REVEALS THAT WOOFU'S
   ENGINEERS ARE DIGGING UP THE TOMB OF TUTANKHAMEW. NOBODY DISTURBS THE PHARAOH'S NAP.`
4. `THE TOMB IS SEALED AGAIN. TUTANKHAMEW ROLLED OVER AND KEPT SLEEPING. MAU FOLLOWS THE DIGGING
   CREWS' TRACKS TO A FACTORY THAT SMELLS OF KIBBLE AND REGRET.`
5. `THE REFINERY IS SCRAP. ITS LAST SHIPMENT WAS ADDRESSED TO "THE GREAT SPHOUND, C/O WOOFU".
   MAU WILL BE DELIVERING IT PERSONALLY.`
6. `THE SPHINX STILL HAS ITS FACE, MOSTLY. THE GRINDER IS SILENT. ON THE HORIZON, A CHROME PYRAMID
   HUMS WITH THE SIGNAL THAT WILL MAKE EVERY CAT IN CATLANTIS SIT.`
7. `THE SERVERS ARE DOWN AND THE UPLINK IS UNPLUGGED. ONE FLOOR UP, WOOFU IS POLISHING HIS
   SARCOPHAGUS. HE SHOULD HAVE POLISHED HIS EXIT STRATEGY.`
