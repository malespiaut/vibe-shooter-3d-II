# Pitch: Vibe Shooter 3D II, Episode 1 "Paws of Fury"

## Hook

Catlantis, a sun-baked parody of ancient Egypt run by cats, has been occupied overnight by the
**Kennel Dominion**: cybernetic dog-men who bolted servo-legs and laser snouts onto themselves and
now want to rebuild the Great Sphinx as the Great Sphound. Their overlord, **Pharaoh-Overlord Woofu**,
sits in a chrome pyramid broadcasting obedience commands. You are the one guard who was napping in a
sunbeam when the invasion happened, and you are very, very annoyed. Eight levels of fast,
readable, keys-and-doors shooting take you from the sandstone city gates, through museums, docks,
tombs and dog-food refineries, to Woofu's throne.

## Player character

**Sergeant Mau**, Royal Whisker Guard, a short-tempered ginger tabby with a notched left ear.
Never speaks in game; reacts through the status-bar face (ears, whiskers, pupils). Mau's whole
arsenal is Catlantean temple tech pressed into service: ankhs that shoot, sistrums that rattle out
bolts, scarabs that explode.

## Tone

Deadpan pulp action with pun-heavy names. The world takes itself seriously (blood, gibs, dark
tombs); the names, signage, intermission texts and Mau's face carry the jokes. Dog-men are menacing
first and funny second: their silhouettes and attacks must read as threats. No pop-culture quotes,
no memes, no fourth-wall jokes in levels.

## Design pillars

1. **Readable at 320x240.** Every enemy, pickup, key and door is identifiable at a glance by
   silhouette and dominant colour ramp. Every enemy attack is telegraphed. If you died, you could
   have seen it coming.
2. **Every level adds one idea.** A new enemy, weapon or mechanic per level (see `levels.md`),
   taught by doing, then remixed with what came before. No level is "more of the same".
3. **Sandstone to steel.** The episode's art direction is a story: levels 1-3 are Egyptian with dog
   scaffolding bolted on, 4 is the pure-Egyptian tomb breather, 5-7 are the industrial occupation
   swallowing Catlantis, 8 is chrome-plated Egyptian kitsch. Palette use shifts with it (warm
   yellows/browns to steel grays and cyan).

## One playthrough (target 60-90 min, normal difficulty "Tomcat")

Title, a two-screen intro, then level 1: about 3 minutes of pistol and claws against slow Mutt
Troopers, learning doors, a key and a secret without a single line of text. Each following level is
4-10 minutes. Intermission tallies (kills, items, secrets, time vs par) and a short joke text sit
between levels. The difficulty climbs steadily with two breathers (level 4, the tomb, and the first
third of level 6). Levels 7 and 8 are the climax: every enemy type mixed, three keys, a level that
rewires itself, then a short gauntlet and the boss arena against Woofu's sarcophagus mech. Ending
text, credits, back to title. Sum of par times is 48:30; a first-time player takes about 1.5x par.
